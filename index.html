How to Play

Music and recording are paused.

In FX mode, the four circles become Reverb, Delay, Filter and Quit, each one a small screen showing the selected instrument. Reverb is frosted glass: more reverb fogs it up, and droplets wipe clear streaks that slowly fog over again. Delay starts faint, with echo pulses travelling out to the rim on every delay beat; the pattern moves in steps and leaves fading after-images, and at 100% it fills the circle. Filter rolls a soft wall of smoke down from the top, with embers drifting out of it: everything inside it turns soft and muffled; at 100% the whole circle is covered. At 0% each effect circle is empty. Pinch in Quit to return to the tracks.
